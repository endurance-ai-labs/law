The problem

Every one of these is answerable from data
the firm already owns.

It sits in eleven systems instead of one. That is the entire problem, and it is a solvable one. Each question below is a working screen in the demo.

01
Which of our top twenty-five clients are shrinking?
Ledger collections joined to CRM engagement recency. Fourteen of the top twenty-five are below prior year — no single partner sees it, because each belongs to a different relationship partner.
02
What is our real margin on fixed-fee work?
Hours and rates from practice management, cost rates from payroll, overhead allocated. Not billings — margin. Most firms genuinely do not have this number.
03
How much did we lose to invoice lines the client rejected and we never appealed?
Portal rejections by reason code, dollars at risk, appeal outcomes. Firms appeal the big ones and let the rest go, because finding them costs more than most are worth.
04
How many days of cash are sitting in unbilled WIP older than ninety days?
WIP and AR aged by matter, partner and client, with the escalation ladder attached. Five days of lockup on this book is $5.6M of working capital.
05
Which associates were under target last quarter while their group turned work away?
Hours dispersion against forward demand from open matters and weighted pipeline. The answer changes staffing decisions, not just reports.
06
Can we prove to a client auditor that the ethical wall held?
Walls enforced at the data layer, not by convention. A screened matter does not grey out — it ceases to exist for that user, and every total recomputes without it.

How it works

Feed → Engine → Exhibit

Your systems stay the systems of record. We end the exporting, rekeying and reconciling between them.

01

Feed

Read-only connections to what you already run — practice management, the DMS, the client portals, docketing, payroll, Microsoft 365 metadata. Nothing is replaced, and if you change any one of them later the platform re-points to the new one.

02

Engine

One computed model of the firm. Realization, margin, lockup, utilization, capacity and compliance derived once, so a number that appears on two pages is the same number — not two calculations that happen to agree.

03

Exhibit

Dashboards, registers, approval chains, e-signature, the board pack and the firm brain. Every figure drills to its source record and shows its derivation on hover.

Governance

Confidentiality is the design constraint,
not an afterthought.

A law firm is the hardest possible environment for a data platform. That is the reason to build one properly rather than to avoid it.

Ethical walls at the data layer

Screened matters are removed before any figure is computed. They do not appear greyed out; they are absent, and every total recomputes without them. Access attempts are logged and provable to a client auditor.

Role-scoped to the real org

The managing partner and the COO see the firm. A practice group leader sees their group and no other group's economics. An associate sees their own matters and hours and no margin at all. Clients see only themselves.

Approval chains with e-signature

Rate exceptions, write-offs, bill release, trust disbursement and new-business intake each move through a named chain. Every step carries a name, a title and a timestamp — with a nudge to whoever is holding it up.

You own it outright

Code, data and infrastructure sit in your own cloud tenant. No per-seat licensing, no vendor data-breach exposure, no account-rep handoff — which is also the cleanest answer to a client's outside counsel guidelines on where their data may sit.
